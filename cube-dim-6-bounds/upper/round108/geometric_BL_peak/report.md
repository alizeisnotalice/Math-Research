nonzero a_v use u_v=a_v/|a_v| and c_v=|a_v|²/32. Geometric BL, with

Jensen gives E[Z log Z]<=r log(2+2r/3). Zero projected columns are
removed from the BL index set; they contribute zero to Z log Z.

identity. BL itself is classical. The new research content here is its
