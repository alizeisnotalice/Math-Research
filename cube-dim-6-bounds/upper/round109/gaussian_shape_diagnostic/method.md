# R109: finite Gaussian-shape diagnostic, not a floor certificate

This is the original body32 equal-weight Cauchy model. Let independent Y_i~Gamma(1/2,1), R=sum Y_i, P_i=Y_i/R. Then R~Gamma(16,1) independently of P~Dirichlet(1/2,...,1/2), and Sigma=A(P)/(4R), A(P)=sum P_i^(-1)vv^T. Starting from the same Gaussian density with covariance2t^2Sigma, the time coefficient is C=Gamma(5/2)/(4pi^3). Its degree−1/2 matrix scaling integrates R exactly, giving

G(x)=K E_P[d(A) Q_A(x)^(-5/2)], d(A)=det(A)^(-1/2),
K=2C Gamma(33/2)/Gamma(16)
 =3*32!/(8*4^16*16!*15!*pi^2).

The two formulas for K are independently checked numerically before any sample. All frame weights and the common Cauchy time remain unchanged. No Gaussian generator/source substitution is made. At uniform P=1/32, A=1024I and the conditional shape integrand is K/(32|x|^5); each worker checks this deterministic identity before sampling.

For each shape and new fixed point x_j, the program computes the identity-reflection value f_P(x_j), and its full64-sign average F_P(x_j). The body32 scale law is invariant under these signs, so both expectations equal the same G(x_j)/K before multiplication by K. A group average reduces the second moment in the extended sense by Jensen; this does not prove the second moment is finite. Empirical variance and chunk variability are diagnostic only, without deterministic error bounds or confidence guarantees.

The mechanism under investigation is B_orbit=K E_P[inf_cube F_P]. With just16 fixed points, m_grid(P)=min_j F_P(x_j) is an **upper** envelope of the true orbit minimum. Its sample mean cannot be used as a g lower bound. The gap between min_j mean(F_P(x_j)) and mean(min_j F_P(x_j)) measures the order-of-minimization loss on this one fixed finite table. For a future proof one still needs a lower enclosure for the continuous-in-x shape minimum, then deterministic control of the Dirichlet shape integral and its boundary tails. The exact per-shape original corner minimum is included only as an internal comparison with the already proved106 ceiling, not as a new kernel baseline or a competing proof.
