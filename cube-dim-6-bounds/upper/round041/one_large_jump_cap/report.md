# A041: a fully paid short-time S1 cap bound

A new positive parameter decomposition gives a true finite-time bound
on the representative positive-face S1 cap. For

    C={x=(1,y), y in[0,1]^5: dist(x,span(1,...,1))<1/64},
    G_short(x)=integral_0^(1/1000) p_t(x) dt,

the exact certificate proves

    ||G_short||_L^(6/5)(C)
       <1948138674853/500000000000000000
       =3.896277349706e-6.

All complementary parameter events are included. This is a local short
potential estimate, not a complete Green cap estimate, I certificate, g
certificate or C6 improvement. The time range above1/1000 and the rest of
the face remain unpaid. The registered C6 upper bound stays64.

## New mechanism and the retained remainder

Split the actual all-ones Cauchy parameter into the disjoint events
|a_ones|>=1/2 and |a_ones|<1/2, with every parameter at the same time t.
There is no assumption that only one parameter is large. On the first
event only this one density is capped; its true line coarea factor is
1/sqrt(6). The original exact Q5 peak then gives

    P_t(x)<=R1/(32pi^6) * t^-4/(1/4+t^2).

The coefficient after dropping the denominator to1/4 is R1/(8pi^6),
exactly1/256 of the old whole-face Cf coefficient. This improvement
applies only to P_t. Applying it to all p_t would omit the second event
and would be invalid.

For that second event, x-a_ones·ones lies a fixed positive distance from
the origin, every other body line and every plane generated by two other
body lines. With eta=sqrt(6)/64, those distances are bounded below by

    c0=sqrt(6)/2-eta,
    c1=sqrt(10/3)/2-eta,
    c2=1/sqrt(2)-eta.

Apply three positive deletions only to the other31 parameters. At stage
k=0,1,2, the projected Gram is

    32I_W-(P_W ones)(P_W ones)^T,

which lies between26I_W and32I_W. It is not assumed tight. The actual
parameter counts31,30,29 give time coefficients992,960,928. All Gram
coarea factors remain present.

Crucially, the ones parameter is kept with its original density throughout
these deletions. After moving the complementary-event denominator bounds
outside its integral, extend that positive integral from |a|<1/2 to all R.
This restores exactly the original three-direction quotient Q3. If ones
lies in the deleted triple span, its quotient projection is zero and the
identity follows from integral f_t=1. There is no fourth deletion, Q2
substitution, changed time, or dropped collection of events.

All26970 ordered non-ones triples are paid. Their five unordered counts
are435,1740,435,580,1305, giving

    C_rem=44743079/(2970pi^6),
    B_t(x)<=C_rem/
       [(c0^2+992t^2)(c1^2+960t^2)(c2^2+928t^2)].

In particular B_t<=32.531406230075 on C, uniformly for every t>0. This
bounded remainder is derived from the actual complementary parameter
event; it is not the old singular common-distance bound evaluated on C.
The full proof is in `theorem.md`; `pointwise_coarea_note.md` supplies an
explicit a.e.-to-everywhere justification for its line-coarea identity.
