## 2. A positive source event on every far fiber

Set alpha=1/32 and beta=rho/(60sqrt(6)). On the fiber sum_v v a_v=x in F,
at least three parameters have |a_v|>beta: if at most two did, removing
them leaves at most30 terms of norm at most sqrt(6)beta, so x is within

one |a_v|>=alpha. Since alpha>beta, the largest parameter leaves at least
two others above beta. These are deterministic necessary conditions, not
conditional independence assumptions on a zero-probability fiber.

Choose the least label attaining max_v|a_v|. This partitions the source
space, including all ties. In branch i solve a_i=1-sum_(j!=i)a_j in the
first-coordinate coarea formula; its Jacobian is1 since all v_i1=1.
Let M31=max_(j!=i)|a_j| and N_beta=#(j!=i){|a_j|>beta}. On this branch,

    |a_i|>=max(alpha,M31), N_beta>=2,
    c_t(a_i)<=c_t(max(alpha,M31)).                         (1)
