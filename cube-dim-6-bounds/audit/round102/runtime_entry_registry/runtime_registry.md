## Scope and interpretation

This is a static source index, not a new runtime experiment and not mathematical certification. Thirty exact files were registered before source reads in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md); their byte counts and SHA-256 values are in [source_manifest.json](source_manifest.json), and AST/JSON/text observations are in [static_extraction.json](static_extraction.json). Source programs were not executed. The only source-listing batches were the bounded existence check and the targeted round090 listing; no dependency tree was expanded.

“Hard wall coverage” below means the inspected source has a timer/supervisor structurally active through the stated stages under its stated process/OS assumptions. A quick observed run does not prove that coverage. A reviewer’s PASS is reported with its scope and is not generalized to other entries. No timeout-over-180 event was observed in this task; absence of such an observation does not certify the design.

## Practical lookup rule for future entries

Before proposing a new production wrapper, look up and record: (1) exact source root/path and hash; (2) whether `SIGALRM` is default termination or a catchable handler, and whether a one-shot timer can be consumed; (3) every cancel/rearm location, including `finally`; (4) which process group is owned and bounded; (5) hard deadline coverage through reap, cleanup, receipt write, print, and process exit; (6) actual complete-wall receipt fields and whether they describe preparation, checker, child, or outer harness; (7) the exact 082 source/review conditions being inherited. Missing any field stays `unknown`/unverified. A runtime design PASS never certifies mathematical content or transfers automatically to another program.
