## Identity and status cautions

- M-C32-3/2 in 067 is the same full model as 006/008. Treating 067's comparison/accounting as a new kernel mechanism would duplicate work.
- M-C38-1(1,2/45) in 018 is the exact 38-column body32-plus-axes model already registered in 012. Its point is the B015 point; it is not a newly discovered frame. Its strict failure is candidate-local.
- Body32 positive-time band/Taylor and same-plane tube machinery in 015 is an interface/coverage mechanism for M-C32-1, not a new stochastic frame. At that snapshot, every face leaf still needed a paid lower bound; unclosed leaves invalidate global (m).
- “Fixed-point” (a single (x)), “fixed-time/cutoff”, “all-time” (all positive (t)), and “all-space/all-cube” are separate scope labels. A point result cannot pay an all-cube infimum; a fixed (T) candidate cannot be described as an all-(T) result; a model-cost diagnostic is not an actual (C_6) bound.
- Old run history may be reported as historical evidence only when the cited archived report says it ran. This registry performed no old run and authorizes none.
