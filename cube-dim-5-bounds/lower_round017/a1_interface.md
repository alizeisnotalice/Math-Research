# Fixed-angle first-axis event interface

Keep the rotated tail plane, s=(1+u)/2, the two other tail axes and unit
masses fixed. Vary only a in[3/2,2], at the same angle(4/5,3/5). The frozen
comparison target remains the complete C_cert, not a rational display.

For the initial finite family, every original triple(l1,l4,l5) in1..4 and
every n in1..16 must be present before pruning:1024 raw cases. With
K=l1*l4*l5 and N=K*n, the common radius r=N^(1/5)/2 is independent of a.
The physical planar capture set and the two tail receiving intervals are
also independent of a. A case's first-axis interval is

    [max(0,j_max*a-r), min(a/2,j_min*a+r)],

where j_min,j_max are the canonical consecutive-block extrema. Hence its
raw first-axis endpoint functions are affine in a. The catalog includes
all four alternatives, even when some are redundant or the case is empty.

On an open interval where every raw affine endpoint has fixed order,
all branch and emptiness decisions are fixed. Each first-axis strip has
affine length, and the union cross section in the remaining four coordinates
is constant. The finite union volume, and therefore its ratio8V/25, is
affine in a on that interval. The finite union volume is continuous because
all box endpoints are continuous. Once all raw pair events and their order
are certified, the finite-family maximum occurs at an event or a window
endpoint. This is a finite-family statement; no uniform all-scale
completeness is inherited from the earlier fixed-a proof.

The first diagnostic processes only one slope-pair event stream, slopes0
and1, after building the full raw catalog. It does not claim complete event
exhaustion or evaluate a new union. Fifth powers are removed from radius
representations, and events are stored as formal sums of radical terms
with outward rational bounds. Any overlapping nonidentical expressions
remain unresolved clusters; no unproved radical independence is assumed.

Uniform geometric screens may use the shortest first-axis span at a=3/2
and the unchanged tail spans. A previously evaluated planar zero remains
zero throughout the window. A case skipped earlier because its unrotated
geometry was empty has an unevaluated planar set, not a certified planar
zero: such cases are explicitly listed for future work.

The receiving-period capacity is a*s^4. Its exact comparison with C_cert
is only a necessary condition for improvement. A separate uniform large-side
capacity uses the same Minkowski argument as before, with numerator a=2
and denominator a=3/2; if its bound at side12 is below1, all larger sides
are uniformly excluded. That does not make the1024-case finite family
complete over this parameter window. No parameter grid or full union is
launched in this diagnostic.
