# R070 numerical observables and conditional candidates

The H2 batch checks a finite numerical sample only, with one-sided derivative at capture kinks. The finite-width `width` run is the surrogate n=2^L; `width_e` separately uses n=exp(L). Neither gives an operator theorem. The reported Cantelli probabilities in both width runs concern concentration of ΣH2 about its own mean. They are NOT source-face retention probabilities for V=Σφ and do not support the covering argument. The bridge H2-good-target to V-face variance is a separate theoretical claim requiring independent proof.

In width_e the denominator is carried as a separate rho*(J−1) correction; extremely small corrections may round away at 90 decimal digits. Retained outputs have no interval guarantees.

Frontier4 is a separate conditional scalar model with complete costs as registered, not a verified Cn bound. Its 120-digit S64 recheck checks selected arithmetic only; other finite guards and the operator theorem are external. `frontier4_per_L.json` records the best retained candidate at each sampled L and all model parameter identities. The reported numerical optimum is only within the finite float-screened set.
