# K35B: ambient row and column mean kernel

These 14 computations concern averages under the **full ambient shell measure**. They do not certify a pointwise degree on a selected good set, a lower bound for conditional degrees, or a new operator inequality. No seven-dimensional computation is involved.

For each target L0∈{64,128,256,512,1024,4096,16384}, set n=2^ceil(L0/log 2), L=log n, x=L−3log L and t=n/x. A row fixes the negative-face radius r=exp(1/√x), and integrates the exact ambient shell-averaged full-capture coefficient G(r,q) over ρ=tq from r to t. A column fixes q=exp(−1/√x), and integrates the same average over radii 1≤r≤tq. Both results are normalized by √x/n. The fixed-face and fixed-shell values are matched in scaled logarithmic coordinates; they are not extrema over radii and do not include subtraction of the original captured corner.

For M=1−(r−q)²/(4rρ), the exact average coefficient is

\[
G(r,q)=\tfrac12M^{n-1}+
\tfrac{n-1}{2}(1+q/r)\bigl(1-(r-q)/(2\rho)\bigr)M^{n-2}.
\]

The first term is the pinned axis and the second averages the remaining independent uniform coordinates. This formula was checked symbolically in the earlier `s35a_symbolic.json`; r/q determines G because ρ=tq. With these matched scaled positions the row and column domains have the same length and identical integrands. Their numerical agreement is therefore expected and is not independent confirmation of a pointwise row/column identity.

For context, the formal fixed-scaled-coordinate Gaussian kernel e^{−(1+v)²/4} has integral √π erfc(1/2)≈0.849892; the table suggests approach to that value, but this numerical run does not prove convergence or a uniform bound. Each case uses 64/128/256 Gauss–Legendre nodes over the entire finite domain, mapped by z=v/(1+v). The 128-to-256 normalized differences are around 2×10⁻¹⁵, but nodes and weights are binary64 and these are **not rigorous interval enclosures**. Products are evaluated with log1p at 90 decimal digits; n is symbolic as 2^m and no length-n array is created.
