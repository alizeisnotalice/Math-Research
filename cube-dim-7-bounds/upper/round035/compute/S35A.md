# S35A: typical-shell expectation diagnostics

This is a numerical diagnostic, not a certified operator bound. For each target L0∈{16,32,64,128,256,1024}, the actual dimension is n=2^ceil(L0/log 2), and L=log n, y=√L, x=L−3log L, t=n/x, β=1+1/t. No length-n vectors are constructed. Fix a negative maximum face with w0=−r, r=1+0.21/(2y), and take the remaining coordinates independent uniform on [−r,r]. The shell is ρ/t∈[1−0.29/L^p,1−0.10/L^p] for p=1/2,3/4,1. The chosen r is one face radius, not an average over the full outer radial interval.

For u=ρ/t and 0<u<1<r, one free-coordinate local normalized intersection length is 1−(|w|−u)_+/(2ρ), with exact mean M=1−(r−u)²/(4rρ). The pinned coordinate has length 1−(r−u)/(2ρ) and exactly one captured face; a free coordinate has expected face count 1+u/r. Consequently the expected full-capture shell coefficient is

\[
G=\tfrac12 M^{n-1}+\tfrac{n-1}{2}(1+u/r)\bigl(1-(r-u)/(2\rho)\bigr)M^{n-2}.
\]

This independently confirms the Sol two-point capacity kernel, including the same-axis term. To compute capture inside D∞, subtract the intersection with the original captured box, not its whole measure: a free coordinate has expected normalized overlap length 1−[1−u+(r−1)²/(4r)+(r−u)²/(4r)]/(2ρ); its positive face is captured with probability (r+u)/(2r). The pinned coordinate contributes no original positive face. Piecewise one-dimensional integrals and the G identity were checked exactly by SymPy in `s35a_symbolic.json`.

These are unconditional face expectations. They do not furnish a lower bound uniformly on R034's good set, nor do they prove asymptotic rates or the two-point capacity theorem. Values for L below the hypotheses of a particular theorem remain diagnostics only. The 64- and 128-node Gauss–Legendre results agree within 10⁻¹¹ on the recorded scaled difference, but are not rigorous quadrature enclosures. In particular, cancellation-safe log1p expressions compute products without storing n coordinates, and exact-coordinate expectations are checked independently of floating quadrature.

The last field in the JSON reports y/L=1/y, the R034 upper estimate for τ/(n/y) when its hypotheses hold; it is not τ itself and is not valid solely on the basis of this numerical experiment.
