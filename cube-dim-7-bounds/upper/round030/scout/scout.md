## Up to eight evidenced losses / routes

1. **Rejected-union cover dilation and cover multiplier remain live.** The finite selection rejects at threshold τ and covers an omitted cube by enlarging a selected (bQ_j) to ((b+1)Q_j), yielding (1+(1+1/t+1/a)^n/τ). Assumptions: finite open cube family, maximal-radius eligible selection, fixed small-cube increments and weights, (t>1,a>2t,b=a(1+1/t)). Evidence: `paper/main.tex:173-218`; R025 `independent_buffer_theory/proof.md:49-76`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:48-57`. **Status:** active; no tighter cover charge is established in these records.

2. **The actual captured eligibility sum is replaced by its ceiling τ.** At a chosen test point, all captured previous contributions lie in the selection sum and are bounded wholesale by τ; the proof does not retain how far below τ that partial sum is. The self contribution is separately bounded by (a^{-n}). Assumptions: last-index tie rule among minimum-radius contributors and (t>1,a>2t). Evidence: `paper/main.tex:243-250`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:59-77`. **Status:** active slack in the current budget. This is already a *joint captured/uncaptured payment*: captured terms are charged to τ, uncaptured terms to the cap region; see item 3. Do not propose that split as new.

3. **Uncaptured terms are paid through an expanded geometric cap, then maximized at a comparison corner.** Actual disjoint increments are embedded in a larger region using only containment and (r_j^{-n}le m(z)^{-n}); the resulting cap integral (H(u;c,t)) is maximized at (u=α\mathbf1), even though the endpoint need not be attainable by a selected center. This produces (J_a(t)) and, after (a\to\infty), the cube-cap subtraction in (B_n(t)). Assumptions: (t>1,a>2t,c>1), actual increment disjointness, coordinatewise monotonicity of (H). Evidence: `paper/main.tex:229-287,351-369`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:79-109,111-168`; `paper/main.tex:735-738`. **Status:** residual, explicitly identified relaxation; the paper says it has not shown an actual selection family can approach the corner budget.

4. **The residual shell budget still discards source/radius/position correlations.** R025 used only containment and disjointness, giving (1+n\log t); R027 strictly improves it by subtracting a ball cap and captured shell integral, but a positive residual remains: (B_n(t)=1-h(t)^n+n\log t-(n/2)\int_1^t\ell_t^{n-1}\,d\rho/\rho). The paper identifies preserving increment origin, radius and position constraints as necessary to improve further. Assumptions: disjoint small increments and the radial comparison geometry; no disjointness of the dilated stars is assumed. Evidence: R025 `independent_buffer_theory/proof.md:90-108`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:187-191`; `paper/main.tex:101-109,735-738`. **Status:** R025’s crude budget is already improved; the remaining R027 budget relaxation is not removed.

5. **The cover factor and pointwise load bound are combined separately.** The final weak estimate first bounds the full finite-cover volume by a cover multiplier times Σv_j, then bounds λΣv_j using a worst-case pointwise load. The current proof does not keep a joint ledger linking the rejected-union part to the same points/terms in the load estimate. Evidence: `paper/main.tex:193-218,371-384`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:50-57,170-176`. **Status:** a potential accounting loss visible in the factorization; no joint cover/load estimate is proved here.

6. **Finite expansion parameter (a) cannot improve this fixed budget family.** At fixed (t), every finite (a>2t) has (A_{n,a}>A_n) and (E_{n,a}>B_n); the two-parameter infimum equals the (a\to\infty) family. Assumptions: the current cap/cover budget and its exact identity for (E_{n,a}-B_n). Evidence: `paper/main.tex:518-537`; asymptotic proof `revision_v3/asymptotic/proof.md:161-183`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:178-185,221-227`. **Status:** explicitly excluded within this budget family only; changing the selection or budget is not excluded.

7. **Threshold rescaling is an algebraic dead end, while τ itself is already optimized exactly.** Rescaling weights to make the eligibility threshold 1 changes the witness charge and gives the same (A+B+τ+AB/τ); optimizing τ gives τ=√(AB). Evidence: R023 `threshold_selection_theory/proof.md:106-114`; paper `main.tex:410-412`; R027 `cube_cap_theory/proof.md:34-38`. **Status:** pure normalization and scalar-τ optimization routes are closed. Any remaining slack must change a geometric budget/selection step, not just units or τ.

8. **Coefficient (1) is a floor only for the present (U_n) formula family under (t)-optimization.** The asymptotic proof establishes (U_n=n\log n-n\log\log n+O(n\log\log n/\log n)) and hence λim sup (C_n/(n\log n)\le1). The paper notes that (B_n(t)\le1+n\log t) alone already yields the leading coefficient 1, so cap subtraction chiefly improves finite-dimensional constants; it explicitly rules out lowering the leading coefficient below 1 by optimizing (t) in this fixed family. Evidence: `revision_v3/asymptotic/proof.md:86-123`; `paper/main.tex:122-134,735-745`; literature comparison `revision_v3/literature/comparison.md:8-9,62-64`. **Status:** (t)-optimization alone is closed; no lower bound for (C_n), and no impossibility result for a changed covering mechanism is claimed.
