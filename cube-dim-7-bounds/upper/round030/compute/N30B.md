# N30B: two-region cap certificates

Assuming the general-σ two-region theorem in the geometry report `geometry_theory/REPORT.md` §3.1.3, these exact certificates give C_n < U listed below. The theorem requires 1/t<σ<1, B>D, and P−D>√(A(B−D)); the entire D is deducted only under this strict condition. For each row the code proves P_lower−D>√(A(B_upper−D))_upper, new F_upper < the full old F_lower at the same t, and new F_upper < U < the original table's full F_lower. For n=7 it also compares U directly to the full round028 Arb lower endpoint. These are improvements to upper bounds, not lower bounds or statements of optimality.

Each t is the fixed original rational in `n30b_certificates.json`; no t optimization is performed. Search uses 55 floating evaluations per dimension to find a sign-change bracket for R=tσ and selects a dyadic R retreated by 1% from the bracket; neither uniqueness nor optimality is claimed. Exact certification is separate and does not rely on the floating search result. It independently expands ℓ^(n−1) as a polynomial in 1/ρ, integrates its constant term as log(1/σ), and bounds the log by a rational atanh series with explicit tail. Exact arithmetic and integer-square-root bounds yield outward B, P, F and τ intervals. One 80-digit positive-integral quadrature per selected point independently checks the expanded P; this is a diagnostic, not the certificate. Decimal roundings in this table are not the interval endpoints; full exact rationals are recorded in JSON.

The original frozen construction uses σ=5/(3t), hence tσ=5/3, and **R=7/4 as a separate guard**. This is valid because R>tσ. My earlier claim of a parameter inconsistency was incorrect: it conflated the guard with the limiting value tσ. `n30b_frozen_guard.py` verifies the requested frozen example with this exact guard, including the coarse inequality P_R≥B−1−7 log R and D=61741/(2187t⁷). The earlier `n30b_frozen.json` separately explores R=tσ at 5/3 and a different σ with tσ=7/4; its own mismatch description and the corresponding comment in `n30b.py` are retracted. The fixed-t optimized table uses R↓tσ in the limiting expression.

For all seven rows, an additional exact finite-a certificate now fixes a=2^512 and guard R=tσ+2^−80. It proves the geometric guard 1+σ/R+2/(a+1)<αβ, the budget α^−n P∞(R)−τ>α^−nD, and the full finite scalar cost < U. Here α=a/(a+1), τ is a rational upper bound for √(A_a E_upper), and the finite tail dominates α^−n P∞(R). The n=7 check ran first; the other six ran in parallel. Exact rounded cost bounds, checks, PIDs and resource measurements are in `n30b_finite_n7.json` and `n30b_finite_other.json`. The proof of the geometry and finite tail remains in the separate geometry report.
