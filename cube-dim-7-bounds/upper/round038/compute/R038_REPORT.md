# R038 Phase A: exact nonedge payment balancing

This finite run revisits exactly 79 cached R037 payment patterns previously labeled H_FAIL. It does not generate 240 new geometries, rerun original atoms or selection, or assert an operator bound. It restores each historical geometry with the frozen R037 generator, reconstructs h and all maximal witness cliques, reads the original q and U, and verifies Σh_iq_i=U and the original exact load before changing any payment. R037's geometry-generation/tie deviations from its initial SPEC remain part of these cases' identities.

For each nonedge pair, the exact pair move preserves P=h_iq_i+h_jq_j, minimizes true clique load and then minimizes E=Σh_iq_i² on the load-minimizing plateau. At every step all nonedge pairs are considered, except pairs with zero combined payment which cannot change; candidates are ranked by (Lnew, ΔE, i, j). A step is accepted only when (Lnew, ΔE)<(Lold,0). There are at most 128 accepted steps per pattern. Each result contains the exact pre/post q, geometry, h, complete clique list, every accepted move, source identity, PID and resources. These are exact finite calculations, not a global minimum certificate.

The batch produced 79 complete patterns, of which 62 have strictly smaller exact load. Fifty stop after a full nonedge scan with no further lexicographic improvement; 29 stop at the 128-step limit and cannot be called stationary. At A/0/6 partial, one pair move reduces the load from approximately 3.94191593×10⁻⁵ to 3.05180438×10⁻⁵, overcoming the particular R037 clique-compression zero-step obstruction. It does not follow that two-point balancing always achieves the global LP optimum.

Using the old R037 scalar LP intervals only as **unverified historical diagnostics**, 23 final patterns satisfy the half-gap comparison and 56 do not. These counts are not 79 new LP certifications. After the full phase, the §7 selection rules deterministically selected at most three cases and `phasea_cert3.py` reran the full mass LP on their saved cliques. For each, numerical LP proposals were converted to rational primal/dual candidates and repaired; every constraint and objective was checked exactly. All three prove the original gap eligibility and strictly fail the half-gap condition for the attained final loads, with independent exact audit PASS:

The comparison uses Mlo≤M≤Mhi and U/Mhi≤Lopt≤U/Mlo; the exact vectors and strict inequalities are in `lp_cert_phasea_*.json`. Failure after 128 steps says only that this limited run did not achieve the half-gap goal. Coordinate stopping is a pair-coordinate condition, not global optimality. The three candidates were selected by historical scores, which served solely as a selection rule.

Actual production used a 15-process dynamic queue. Recorded wall was about 328.33 s, summed per-task CPU about 1691.02 s, with 15 distinct worker PIDs and peak per-worker RSS about 92 MB. An early live sample showed 15 active workers between 87.7% and 95.4% CPU; later the queue naturally drained to four busy workers. Logs, registration, hashes and exact case outputs remain locally; all 79 case files together occupy about 71 MB. No computation was prolonged to fill the machine.

The actual Phase A registration and source hashes were frozen before execution in `phasea_registration.json`. The theoretical SPEC by the soft agent was completed after launch; a subsequent read-only check confirmed the implemented pair/plateau rule matches it. The phase selection/LP script and its three case hashes were separately frozen after the 79 outputs completed, before the three LP calls. No old geometry/atom/selection/flow or baseline LP was rerun in Phase A; reconstructing the frozen geometry and clique graph is metadata recovery, not a new independent sample. Source code, original per-case hashes, exact scores, proof vectors and all output hashes are retained in the private workspace.

## Independent audit and compact archival replay

The independent `cube-r030-num-audit` Phase A audit reports 79/79 PASS: it reconstructed each witness graph and complete cliques, checked every recorded payment/load/energy step, and independently scanned all nonedges at the 50 coordinate stops. It does not claim that each intermediate chosen pair was globally lex-best. Its separate three-LP audit verified all rational primal/dual constraints and strict half-gap failures. The scope, retries and source hashes are in the independent audit reports.

`phasea_compact.json.gz` retains each exact geometry, h, U, q-before, full cliques, accepted pair indices, stop state, before/after load and canonical final-q hash. `compact_manifest.json` maps all 79 original raw SHA-256 digests and the three LP source/certificate hashes. It is a compact lossless record of the data required for the replay, not a byte-identical compression of the original 71 MB raw results: the repeated per-step fractions and full final q are intentionally reconstructed. The full raw files remain untouched in the private clone.

`replay_compact.py` reconstructs the rational pair steps without rerunning all-pair candidate selection, verifies true clique loads, exact payment conservation, strict lex decrease and every final q hash. It also independently checks all constraints of the three saved primal/dual LP certificates, exact objectives and H-failure inequalities from the compact geometry/clique data. The local replay PASS covered all 79 cases and 3827 steps with no optional terminal rescan. `--terminal` enables an additional full nonedge check for the 50 stationary results but was not run in this archival task; the independent audit already performed that scan. The compact replay itself does not establish that every chosen pair was best among the original full candidate set. Suggested archival white list and required provenance are in `ARCHIVE.md`.
