# R062 independent layer arithmetic audit

Seven source files were hashed and registered before the independent checker ran. No producer helper, frontier search or pilot was run. The checker rebuilds all 128 half-open q layers by consecutive exact endpoints from 18/25 to 24/25, their radius bounds with trim 2^-30, and the R062 error terms. For each row it verifies both 48-term atanh endpoints, the exact positive remainder bound, and containment in an independently reconstructed 40-term atanh interval; it separately checks binary floors and ceilings at 2^-200 and 2^-160, including small negative values. It then reconstructs μ upper, the ceiling-to-10^-6 plus 10^-4 definition of χ, η>χ, σ=3/50 margins, each positive gamma contribution and their sum. All recorded fractions match.

The total gamma lower bound is approximately 0.21456847774483484 and exceeds 429/2000 by approximately 0.0000684777448. The smallest eta and sigma margins are approximately 0.00212633 and 0.00020764. The post-run exact arithmetic is 429/2000−3/47=14163/94000, and subtracting 3/20 gives 63/94000. These are conditional scalar statements.

The 512-layer frontier top certificate is a **different ideal model**, without the fixed candidate's finite-error corrections or radius trims. Its 512 retained rows were all independently recomputed, and its μ<η and positive c_model lower bound were verified; c_model_lower≈0.1555301881 and minimum η−μ≈2.53382e−6. The audit does not establish an operator theorem, frontier completeness, or continuous optimum. Exact outputs, source/registration hashes, PID, CPU, elapsed time and RSS are in `audit.json`.
