# R044 independent finite-certificate audit

This audit uses Python stdlib Fraction and imports no producer helper. It uses the independent R038 Phase A result files to authenticate the complete clique sets and exact source hashes, and verifies that each compact file reproduces the same geometry, budgets, initial q, union payment and cliques. It does not solve LPs or replay the old geometric atoms or the producer scans.

Two B candidates pass direct exact reconstruction. C/1/13 uses coordinates [1,3,9]; C/4/07 uses [1,13,16]. In both, all other coordinates are unchanged, all new q are nonnegative, the original and new full payments equal U, and the actual maximum of the entire frozen clique list decreases strictly, by approximately 16.6666666657% relative to the original load. `candidates.json` contains exact endpoints, gap, old/new coordinates and source hashes. These are feasible moves, without an optimality or exhausted-search claim.

For all 76 main cases the recorded primal m and dual z are nonnegative, every primal vertex inequality and every dual clique inequality is satisfied, and the recorded objectives agree with their exact recomputation. Thus M_lower≤M≤M_upper and U/M_upper≤Lopt≤U/M_lower. Crucially, **all 76 exact objective gaps are strictly positive**. Their relative widths range approximately from 3.03e−174 to 4.66e−10; floating rounding to zero does not establish equality. Each full global proposal is nonnegative and payment-conserving; recomputation of the true clique load confirms precisely 67 strictly improve, and 9 do not. Exact M endpoints and hashes are recorded per case in `lp_batch.json`.

The raw initial proposal ledger labels 2432 entries `lp_failed`; retry ledger labels 2418 `exact_feasible` and 14 `rationalization_infeasible`. The logged failed vectors are absent, so this audit cannot reconstruct their 14 coordinate values without replaying the solver. The producer code associates this label with a negative residual anchor after rationalization. A failed or invalid proposal is not proof the mathematical problem is infeasible. A nonedge coordinate stop from R038 is not an R039 all-pair stop. These finite payment checks do not provide a new C_n bound.

## Incremental C audit

`audit_c.py` independently checks all 38 saved exact candidate vectors against the SHA-identified R038 source and prior 76-case LP audit. It recomputes the true loads L0 and Lglobal and midpoint (L0+Lglobal)/2, total payments, nonnegativity and actual changed-coordinate support, and all unchanged coordinates. Each of the 38 has load strictly below its midpoint. Actual support sizes are 3:5, 4:17, 5:10, 6:2, 8:1, 9:1, 10:1 and 12:1. The five 3-support examples are B/0/11 [0,3,26], B/1/09 [1,2,35], B/2/09 [1,3,42], B/3/10 [0,3,60], and B/4/10 [0,3,67]. Exact values and source hashes are in `audit_c.json`.

Of 76 records, 67 report numerical solver status 0; 38 of those contain the verified exact vectors and 29 contain none. The remaining 9 have no strict certified initial gap and no candidate. Numerical status 0 and its numerical MIP gap do not certify integer optimality. The 29 missing vectors are not counted as exact passes or failures, and lack of an exact vector is not proof of infeasibility. No MILP is run here.
