标题   : Noether's Theorem for Fractional Optimal Control Problems
作者   : Gastao S. F. Frederico; Delfim F. M. Torres
年份   : 2006
出处   : arXiv
被引   : 0
DOI    : (无)
arXiv  : (无)
摘要   : We begin by reporting on some recent results of the authors (Frederico and Torres, 2006), concerning the use of the fractional Euler-Lagrange notion to prove a Noether-like theorem for the problems of the calculus of variations with fractional derivatives. We then obtain, following the Lagrange multiplier technique used in (Agrawal, 2004), a new version of Noether's theorem to fractional optimal control systems.
