摘要   : <jats:p>Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite simple graph with vertex set \(V=V(G)\) and edge set \(E=E(G)\). A vertex \(v \in V\) is edge-vertex dominated by an edge \(e \in E\) if \(e\) is incident with \(v\) or \(e\) is incident with a vertex adjacent to \(v\). An edge-vertex dominating set of \(G\) is a subset \(D \subseteq E\) such that every vertex of \(G\) is edge-vertex dominated by an edge of \(D\).
