# Registration
Three prescribed regular 8D points only: (A,A), (A,B), (B,B), A=(1,1,1,0), B=(1,1,0,0). Reconstruct 4D cone sums from the 8 body directions and 3 base cones under all signed permutations (1152 cone images); group equal node multisets, then add every grouped term into a single rational function before simplification. No deletion of poles per cone. p4(t,x) = pi^-4 times that full sum evaluated at z=-it divided by 16. Multiply paired densities at a common t; integrate over t>=0 via exact real partial fractions in u=t^2 and the standard integral of (t^2+a^2)^-k, provided every denominator factor has rational positive squared pole and the integral is proper. Stop a point if intermediate symbolic terms exceed 200000; no 1152^2 cone expansion. Independently cross-check each resulting integral by 80- and 160-digit mpmath quadrature, and establish boundedness at t=0 and an explicit large-t bound. Values are point anchors, not a global cube minimum, g0 or C8.
