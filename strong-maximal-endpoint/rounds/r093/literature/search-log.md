# Literature search log: strong maximal Solyanik bounds

Checked: 2026-11-11 (UTC date).

## Scope

Target: the unweighted restricted Tauberian/halo function of the uncentered strong maximal operator over axis-parallel rectangles in \(\mathbb R^n\), in the regime \(\alpha\uparrow1\). Main questions were the theorem's dimension dependence, the range in \(\alpha\), and whether a fixed-dimension estimate says anything uniform for dimension \(n=N\to\infty\).

## Sources opened and reading locations

1. **Solyanik, original paper, Russian full text.** MathNet journal record and PDF:
   - Record: <https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mzm2453>
   - PDF: <https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=mzm&option_lang=eng&paperid=2453&what=fullt>
   - Read: PDF pp. 2–6 (printed pp. 83–87), especially printed p.83 notation/theorem, pp.85–87 induction and lower-bound argument. Also checked bibliographic record for translation/DOI.
   - Original is Russian; the PDF text extraction is imperfect, so equations were checked against Hagelstein–Parissis's later English restatement below. The formula and inequality direction in the original theorem page are legible in the rendered PDF.
   - Short identifying excerpt retained in evidence: “for \(n>2\), \(\phi_n(u)-1\sim(u-1)^{1/n}\)” (10 words, formula tokens counted conservatively).

2. **Hagelstein–Parissis, “Solyanik estimates in ergodic theory.”** Publisher-hosted full text:
   - Article page: <https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/colloquium-mathematicum/all/145/2/91594/solyanik-estimates-in-ergodic-theory>
   - PDF: <https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/91594>
   - Read: printed pp.194–196 (PDF pp.2–4), definitions of the geometric strong operator/Tauberian constant and Theorem 1.3; printed p.205 (PDF p.13) for notation; reference list pp.206–207 (PDF pp.14–15).
   - This is a later restatement, not the original proof. It writes the strong estimate as \(C_S(\alpha)-1\lesssim_n(1/\alpha-1)^{1/n}\) for \(\alpha\) sufficiently close to 1 and explicitly defines the subscript \(n\) as dependence of the implicit constant.
   - Short excerpt retained in evidence: “for \(\alpha\) sufficiently close to 1” (7 words).

3. **Hagelstein–Parissis, “Solyanik estimates in harmonic analysis.”** Full text of the published proceedings contribution was opened at:
   - PDF: <https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=fae3e600f08aa9ca11d3d5b1229d27d104e588a1&repid=rep1&type=pdf>
   - Read: printed pp.4–9 (PDF pp.4–9), Tauberian definition, Theorem 1 (attributed to Solyanik), notation definition, and the explicit estimate for the iterated coordinate maximal operator in Theorem 2; checked references on printed pp.17–18.
   - This is a secondary restatement. It confirms \(C_S(\alpha)-1\sim_n(1/\alpha-1)^{1/n}\) as \(\alpha\to1^-\), and explicitly defines \(\sim_n\) by two inequalities with dimension-dependent implicit constants. The same paper gives an explicit all-\(\alpha\) bound for the iterated coordinate maximal operator; via the standard pointwise domination of the geometric strong maximal operator by that composition, this also gives a valid but very coarse dimension-explicit upper bound for \(C_S\).
   - Short excerpt retained in evidence: “the constant \(c\) depends … on the dimension \(n\)” (9 words).

## Search limits

The original English translation is indexed by DOI 10.1007/BF01209086, but the publisher DOI redirect was inaccessible to the search connector. The original Russian journal PDF is available from MathNet and was read directly. The publisher's later English restatement was used to cross-check the formula and notation. No claim is made that this is an exhaustive literature review; the query was deliberately narrow and stopped after confirming the original theorem, its dimension dependence, and the applicable near-one range.
