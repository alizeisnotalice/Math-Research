# The complete equal-weight hyperbolic-cross price at bounded central quantiles

Fix a compact subinterval $J\Subset(0,1)$ of indicator thresholds $p$. For all sufficiently large $n$ (uniform on $J$), and the r093 sources $E_T=\{x\in[-1,1]^n:\sum -\log|x_i|>T\}$, the full-space supremum over $T\ge0$ equals $\exp(s_p)$ where $s_p$ is the deterministic optimal lower hit quantile of $S=\sum Z_{r_i}$. This includes the exterior of the source cube. We give the upper argument specifically because the uniform single-coordinate enlargement in r093 can be too expensive when $p$ is small.

As in r093, reduce actual sides at positive receivers to $[-r_ix_i,x_i]$, $0\le r_i\le1$. Given a receiver with $j$ exterior coordinates $x_i>1$, put $h=\sum_{x_i>1}\log x_i$. Its fraction of the sample inside the source cube is at most $\prod_{x_i>1}2/(1+x_i)\le e^{-h/2}$, hence $h< C=2\log(1/p)$ on a strict success. Condition on lying in the cube. In each exterior direction its sampled interval is now $[-\min(r_ix_i,1),1]$; in each interior direction the original interval is unchanged. Conditional log increments are again independent, all with the same deterministic-action law, with base radii $\min(x_i,1)$. Success therefore requires $\sum_{x_i\le1}-\log x_i>T-s_{p e^{h/2}}$. Each action sum contains an independent unit-rate Gamma of shape $n$, so every density is bounded by $K/\sqrt n$ (absolute $K$, by Stirling at the Gamma mode). For a fixed action and subsequently its supremum, $s_{p e^{h/2}}\le s_p-a h$ with $a=p\sqrt n/(2K)$, whenever the threshold is below one. Uniformly for $p\in J$, $s=s_p=\mu n+O_J(\sqrt n)$ by bounded individual variances, maximal mean, Chebyshev and the matched-mean iid lower example.

For $T<s$, $|E_T|/2^n\ge\Pr(\Gamma(n,1)>s)\ge e^{-s}s^{n-1}/(n-1)!$. The full cube halo contributes at most $2^n\exp(O_J(\sqrt n))$ by r095. The logarithmic price is at most $(\mu-1-\log\mu)n+o(n)<s$. For $T\ge s$, the case $j=n$ cannot succeed. Write $P_d(x)=\sum_{r=0}^d x^r/r!$. Normalized by $2^ne^{-(T-s)}$, the inside halo is at most $P_{n-1}(T-s)$, and the total contribution from $1\le j<n$ is at most
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\binom nj \int_0^C e^{-(a-1)h}P_{n-j-1}(T-s+ah)\,h^{j-1}\,dh/(j-1)! .
\]
Here the external positive-coordinate Jacobian is $e^h$, and the other $n-j$ coordinates yield their genuine Gamma survival. Eventually $a C<s/2$ and $n/(a-1)<s/2$, uniformly on $J$. Dominate the polynomial by its value at $T-s/2$ and integrate the positive kernel to infinity. For each $j$, $\binom nj/(a-1)^j\le(s/2)^j/j!$. Also the interior polynomial is at most $P_{n-1}(T-s/2)$. Their sum is at most $P_{n-1}(T)$ by the exact polynomial translation identity. Multiplying back gives $e^s |E_T|$, including every exterior face. Equal weights and the interior depth limit $T\to\infty$ attain the supremum. All divisions by $2^n$ cancel in the physical price.

At each bounded central standardized level $u=\Phi^{-1}(1-p)$ the unweighted deterministic quantile is $\mu n+\sigma\sqrt n u+O_J(1)$. Here is the short argument: r022's independent-array one-term Edgeworth comparison gives the quantile as $m+u\sqrt V+O_J(1)$ uniformly (all coordinates have bounded exponential moments and $V\asymp n$). Write $D=\mu n-m$, so $|V-\mu n|\le C\sqrt{nD}$. The resulting upper error is bounded by $-D+C_J\sqrt D+O_J(1)$, and choosing the mean-optimal common action gives the reverse. Consequently these complete physical sources satisfy the r110 central criterion with bounded, rather than logarithmic, rank error. No decomposition of a general binary source into this class is assumed.

## Uniform bounded central error for all weights

For $E_{T,w}$ of r093 ($0\le w_i\le1,\ \max w_i=1$), the full price over all depths and weights is between $e^{s_p}$ and $e^{s_p}(1+O_J(e^{-c_J\sqrt n}))$, uniformly for $p\in J$. In particular the same central criterion holds for the full weighted class. Here and below errors in strict inequalities only affect boundaries of null measure. Partition receivers by the set $I$ of coordinates with $\log^+x_i>b$, with a small fixed $b>0$ to be chosen. By the cube hit fraction, $h=\sum\log^+x_i<C_J$ and $|I|\le C_J/b$, so there are polynomially many nonempty strata and just one empty stratum.

Write $D=\sum(1-w_i)$. For $D\le n/4$ the sum has a positive fraction of exponential components with weight at least $1/2$; the characteristic-function bound $(1+c t^2)^{-c'n}$ gives a uniform density upper $K/\sqrt n$. At all central $p\in J$ the quantile for every deterministic action satisfies $\partial_{w_i}q\ge c_J>0$. Indeed the r093 quantile derivative is $E[Z_i f_{-i}(q-w_iZ_i)]/f(q)$; on $1\le Z_i\le2$ the deleted-sum standardized coordinate stays bounded by Chebyshev and the bounded mean/variance moments, the unstandardized local Gaussian density approximation error is $O(1/n)$, and its main density is $\ge c_J/\sqrt n$. The interval has a uniformly positive probability from the unshifted exponential component. Integrating weights toward one at each fixed action and then optimizing gives $s_{p,w}\le s_p-c_J D$. This proof covers zero weights by a one-sided derivative or continuity.

Take $b<\min(c_J/2,\mu/8)$, fixed uniformly on $J$. On stratum $I$ enclose each coordinate in $[0,e^{b_i}]$, with $b_i=b$ off $I$ and $b_i=C_J$ on $I$. The conditional hit condition established above with weights gives $\sum_i w_i(-\log x_i)>T-s_{p,w}+(a-1)h$, since returning from clipped to actual radii costs at most $h$, and $a=p\sqrt n/(2K)$. Put $s'=s_{p,w}-(a-1)b 1_{I\ne\varnothing}$. The log-coordinate substitution $x_i=e^{b_i-U_i}$ bounds the stratum volume/source ratio by
\[
 \exp(\textstyle\sum b_i)\,G_w(T-s'+\textstyle\sum w_i b_i)/G_w(T)
 \le \exp\max\{\textstyle\sum b_i,\ s'+\sum(1-w_i)b_i\}.
\]
Here $G_w$ is the survival of a weighted sum of unit exponentials, extended by one on negative arguments; its shift inequality follows by conditioning on a weight-one component. The first exponent is at most $nb+O_J(1)$. The second is at most $s_p-(c_J-b)D$ on the empty stratum, and at most $s_p-(a-1)b+O_J(1)$ on each remaining stratum. Summing the polynomial number of the latter retains exponential decay in $\sqrt n$.

For $D>n/4$ use conditional threshold $p$ alone, at an extra raw-radii cost $h<C_J$; in the preceding volume comparison set $s'=s_{p,w}+C_J$. The mean is at most $3\mu n/4$ and Chebyshev gives $s_{p,w}\le3\mu n/4+O_J(\sqrt n)$. Both exponents are below $s_p$ by a fixed positive multiple of $n$ even after inclusion of the term $bD+O_J(1)$. This completes the upper for arbitrary weights. The matching lower again comes from letting the equal-weight depth tend to infinity. The result uses a single truncated log halfspace, as opposed to an unrestricted intersection or union of such sources.
