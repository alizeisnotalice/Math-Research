# A central-quantile criterion for the unrestricted endpoint

Let $H_n$ denote the unrestricted real strong indicator constant. Consider the following open statement: for each fixed $A<\infty$, uniformly on $|u|\le A$,
\[
 \log H_n(1-\Phi(u))\le\mu n+\sigma\sqrt n\,u+o_A(\log n).                 \tag{C}
\]
If (C) holds, the coefficient in the unrestricted endpoint $\log C_N$ equals $c_Q$. Thus the critical-window upper problem can be reduced to genuine indicator estimates at bounded standardized quantiles, with no shape restriction on the sections.

Take $L=\log N$, $n=\lfloor N/L^2\rfloor$ and $k=N-n$. For an arbitrary positive finite-volume indicator $E$ in $N$ dimensions let $g(z,y)=M_n1_{E_z}(y)$ and $D_n(b)=1-g^*(|E|e^{\mu n+b})$. Fubini gives the unrestricted sectional weak bound at each threshold using the original source volume. For fixed $A$ and $\epsilon>0$, apply (C) at $u-\epsilon L/(\sigma\sqrt n)$ on $[-A-2,A+2]$. This gives $D_n(b)\ge\Phi(u)-2\epsilon L f_n(b)$ on $|u|\le A+1$, $b=\sigma\sqrt n u$, with $f_n=\Phi'(u)/(\sigma\sqrt n)$. The dimension required may depend on $A,\epsilon$. Outside this compact interval use r091 (6), which gives a global Gaussian CDF lower envelope with rank shift $C_H\log n$, variance time inflation $O(\log n)$ and absolute error $2n^{-4}$. On $|u|\le A_0\sqrt L$ its shift loss is at most $C L f_n(b)+2n^{-4}$ with $C$ independent of $A,\epsilon$; beyond that tube use its absolute tail estimate as in r107.

Choose a smooth $0\le\chi_A\le1$ equal to zero on $[-A,A]$ and one outside $[-A-1,A+1]$, with bounded derivatives through order four independent of $A\ge2$. In the r107 heat subsolution use initial correction $C L f_n\chi_A$ and add $2\epsilon L+C_1$ to the constant rank shift. The compact bound verifies the initial condition where $\chi_A<1$, including its entire transition. Increasing $C_1$ pays the negative constant in $c_Q(u^2-1)$. In the remaining central tube the coarse bound verifies the condition with $C$ sufficiently large. The r107 heat identity for this correction is exact for every such smooth cutoff. Its derivative estimates use only the supremum norms of the cutoff derivatives, the Gaussian factors and $n$ dominating every fixed power of $L$. These facts still hold for the chosen $n$. The exact quadratic cancellation, global tail comparison and $O(1/t)$ one-step fee of r107 therefore propagate the bound to time $N$ with cumulative fee $O(\log(N/n))=O(\log L)$. Only the initial comparison in r107 depended on the geometric section hypothesis.

At the critical test rank the heat correction divided by $CL f_N$ is $E\chi_A(\sqrt{n/N}u_N+\sqrt{1-n/N}G)$ with $G$ standard Gaussian and $u_N=-z_N+O(L/\sqrt N)$. Its limit is at most $P(|G|\ge A)$ since $z_N\sqrt{n/N}\to0$. For any desired rank slack $\epsilon_0 L$, choose $A$ large, then $\epsilon$ small, so the terminal correction and the constant rank shift together cost less than $\epsilon_0 L/2$ for sufficiently large $N$. The strict physical rank comparison yields $\log H_N(p_N)\le\mu N-d_N+(c_Q+\epsilon_0)L$. Let $\epsilon_0\downarrow0$. The r092 reduction and the matching $Q_N$ lower give the assertion for the Orlicz endpoint. This is a sufficient criterion: the $o(\log n)$ remainder in (C) for arbitrary geometric inputs has yet to be proved. The $O(\log n)$ remainder from r091 alone leaves an undetermined coefficient.
